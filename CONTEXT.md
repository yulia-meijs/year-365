# 365 - My Year

A personal year-long experiment for noticing which choices and experiences support recovery and make life feel richer.

## Language

**Year Experiment**:
The 365-day container for the user's recovery, wellbeing, and life observations. It continues for its full date range regardless of individual alcohol records.
_Avoid_: Alcohol-free challenge, sobriety streak

**Personal Day**:
A local calendar date in the user's configured timezone to which daily observations belong. Completed personal days do not move when the timezone changes.
_Avoid_: Entry date, UTC day

**Daily Check-In**:
The single editable summary of body, mind, lifestyle, and life observations for a personal day.
_Avoid_: DailyEntry, habit checklist

**Local Profile**:
The single person's identity and preferences represented on a device during the local-first release.
_Avoid_: User account, authenticated user

**Unknown Observation**:
A measure the user did not record for a personal day. It is distinct from zero, no, symptom-free, or inactive and is excluded from derived claims.
_Avoid_: Empty value, negative observation

**Alcohol-Free Day**:
A personal day on which the user records no alcohol consumption. It is an observation within the Year Experiment, not a condition for continuing it.
_Avoid_: Successful day, clean day

**Activity**:
A meaningful experience the user records to understand its relationship with energy, mood, or enjoyment.
_Avoid_: Task, productivity item

**Project**:
A longer-term subject the user wants to explore through optional milestones, notes, and invested time. Creative work is a project category rather than a separate project type.
_Avoid_: CreativeProject, task list

**Craving**:
A timestamped experience of wanting alcohol, including its intensity, context, response, and outcome.
_Avoid_: Failure, relapse

**Wellbeing Event**:
A timestamped record created when the user feels unwell and wants to capture symptoms and context without receiving a diagnosis.
_Avoid_: Medical incident, diagnosis

**Eligible Observation**:
A recorded measure that satisfies the completeness rules for a specific comparison or summary.
_Avoid_: Data point, filled field
